What this version proposes

  • Breaking points get a way out. The flows ask what the user sees when one happens. Here an unreadable link offers a screenshot or typing the details, an unusable screenshot says what to capture, and conflicting data lets the user choose which source to trust.
  • Both Flow 2 backlog items are in. The result shows the best size with its match percentage, and a slider fine-tunes it for a looser or tighter fit than the item's own.
  • The tape measure is the profile. Every optional onboarding screen visibly lengthens it, and a short tape lowers the top match percentage, so skipping has a cost the user can see.
  • The menu holds the profile and settings. Connecting Zalando imports three sample orders into the purchase history and lengthens the tape. Connecting Atelier Nord, the small boutique, shows the "format can't be read" case, the import-format risk in the concept brief. Your photo is set up under personal details, since the brief puts it under user info in settings. Sizes and units, notifications and language only save the choice for now.
  • Fitting Room and Styles are coming soon. Both tabs show their page blurred, with a swinging hanger and "Coming soon" on top. The user flows only cover Flow 1 and Flow 2, so the tasks test those.
